# Personal Experiments and Research Registry

Experiments store hypothesis, intervention, control/comparison, period, metrics, observations, result and confidence. Example: test whether starting difficult work with 10 easy questions improves completion.

Research Registry stores source, title, authors, year, DOI/URL, finding, limitations, product implication, evidence level and review date.

Evidence levels: EMPIRICAL, RESEARCH_SUPPORTED, HEURISTIC, USER_SPECIFIC, EXPERIMENTAL.

Do not convert a paper directly into a hard-coded rule. Research → assess → hypothesis → configurable implementation → personal measurement → evaluation.
