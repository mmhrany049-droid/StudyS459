# Exam and Goal Engine

School exams and mock exams are first-class events. Store planned vs actual topics separately, plus duration, question count, status and retake/preparation relationships.

Mock post-analysis covers score, accuracy, unanswered, time, topic/difficulty breakdown, errors, prerequisites and planned-vs-actual coverage.

Goals support approximately three months and decompose 3-month → month → week → day → task. Track baseline, target, milestones and multidimensional progress. Adapt future milestones when actual progress deviates.
